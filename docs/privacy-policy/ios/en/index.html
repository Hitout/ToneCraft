ToneCraft iOS App Privacy Policy

Effective: October 18, 2026 | Last updated: October 18, 2026

简体中文

This policy explains how the ToneCraft iOS instrument tuner and metronome uses device permissions and handles information. Contact the developer at gxyancn@qq.com.

1. Microphone and Tuning

When you open the tuner and grant microphone permission, the App analyzes live audio in device memory to calculate pitch and display tuning results. Audio is not saved as a recording or uploaded to our servers. The App stops capture and clears its analysis buffer when you leave the tuner, the App enters the background, permission is lost, or the audio session is interrupted.

2. Flashlight and Haptics

The App requests camera permission only when you enable beat flashes. It controls the flashlight without taking photos, recording video, or accessing your photo library. Beat vibration uses haptic feedback on supported devices. Flashes and haptics run only in the foreground. Denying camera permission does not prevent metronome audio playback.

3. Local Preferences

The App stores your language, last opened page, instrument and tuning choices, tempo, time signature, and other practice settings in local application storage. We do not receive these preferences. Deleting the App removes its local data; system backup and restore may retain or restore preferences according to your Apple device settings.

4. Background Metronome Audio

Beat audio is generated on your device. Playback can continue in the background or while locked. System media controls may display tempo and time signature and provide a stop button. Stopping playback ends beat generation. Background playback does not enable the tuner's microphone.

5. Network Access and Third Parties

This version does not provide accounts, cloud synchronization, advertising, behavioral analytics, third-party crash reporting, in-app purchases, or cross-app tracking. Opening the Privacy Policy or User Agreement uses the system browser to visit GitHub Pages. GitHub, your browser, and Apple system services may handle operational or web access data under their own policies.

If you email the developer, your address and message are used to handle the inquiry, feedback, or privacy request and retained for the necessary period, except where a longer period is required for legal or security reasons.

6. Your Controls

You can revoke microphone or camera permission in iOS Settings at any time. The tuner requires microphone permission, and beat flashes require camera permission. Features that do not need the denied permission remain available. We cannot remotely access tuning audio or preferences stored on your device.

7. Updates and Contact

We will update this page and its date when features or information handling practices change. For privacy questions, contact gxyancn@qq.com.
