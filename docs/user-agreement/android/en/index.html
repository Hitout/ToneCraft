2. Permissions and device features

The tuner requires microphone permission. Live audio is analyzed on your device and is not saved as a recording or uploaded. You may deny or revoke permission in system settings. Without microphone permission, tuning is unavailable; features that do not require it remain available.

The metronome may continue in the background or on the lock screen where the system permits; you can stop it in the App or system media controls. Background beat playback does not activate the tuner microphone.
